The requested feature has already been implemented and its public checks pass.

Read `.eval-skill/refactoring/SKILL.md`. Inspect only the feature diff and its immediate collaborators. Apply at most one behavior-preserving refactor when it reduces change propagation for a concrete concept touched by the feature. Otherwise make no change.
